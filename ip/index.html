Everything is measured in your browser against this site alone. Your address is never written to a log or a database, and there are no ads or third-party trackers here.

Speed figures cover the route between you and this server only. A different server, or a busier moment, gives different numbers, so read them as the size of the change rather than a rating of your connection.

A VPN cannot hide everything. Your screen size, graphics card and browser version stay readable whatever you do, and together they can identify you with no address at all.
