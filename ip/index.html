How to read this

What an IP address is

It is the return address your device gives out so websites know where to send the page back. Anyone you connect to can see it, and your internet provider knows which customer it belonged to at what time.

Why the location looks wrong

Location is guessed from who owns the address block, not from your device. It usually lands on your provider's regional hub, which can be a long way from you. A wrong city is normal and is not evidence of a leak.

What a WebRTC leak is

Browsers have a built-in video calling system that needs to discover your real network address to connect people directly. Any page can quietly ask it for that address, and it answers from outside your VPN tunnel.

Why the timezone matters

Your VPN moves your address to another country, but your operating system clock stays where you are. A site that compares the two sees the contradiction immediately, which flags you as using a VPN and hints at your real region.

This page runs its checks in your browser. Your address is never written to a log or a database, and there are no third-party trackers or advertising scripts on this page.

Results are diagnostic, not a security guarantee. A clean report means these specific checks found nothing, not that you are anonymous.
