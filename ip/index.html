Everything is measured in your browser against this site alone. Your address is never written to a log or a database, and there are no ads or third-party trackers here.

Speed figures cover the route between you and this one server. Download uses three parallel connections, the way dedicated speed tests do; latency is measured to the nearest edge of this site's network. Numbers still move with the moment, so a difference under about ten per cent is noise rather than a finding, and a dedicated speed test with servers next to your VPN exit may read higher than this route does.

A VPN cannot hide everything. Your screen size, graphics card and browser version stay readable whatever you do, and together they can identify you with no address at all.
