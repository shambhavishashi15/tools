What the results mean, and what they do not

The VPN checker reports what a specific set of tests could observe from your browser at the moment you ran them. A clean result means those checks found nothing, not that you are anonymous or secure. It cannot see traffic outside your browser, cannot audit your VPN provider's conduct, and cannot promise any outcome. Do not rely on it as your only safeguard where your safety depends on it.

Speed figures describe one route at one moment, measured against a third-party network. Connections fluctuate, and repeated runs will differ. Treat the numbers as an indication, not a guarantee of service quality.
